Privacy Policy for Laween

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Laween ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Camera Access

Our application requests access to your device's Camera. This is used solely for the purpose of allowing you to take photos for your profile and shared outing experiences. Photos are uploaded to our secure database only when you explicitly choose to share them.

Location Data

Our application requires Location access (including background location) to provide core features such as "Locate Friends" and midpoint calculation. We prioritize your privacy with our "Ghost Mode" guarantee: you can choose to be invisible on the map at any time, and your location is only shared with approved group members during active sessions.

Biometric Data

We use device-level biometric authentication (Face ID/Fingerprint) for secure login. We do not collect or store your biometric data on our servers; authentication happens entirely on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core location-based services (Midpoint calculation, Friend tracking).
  • To enable social features (Group chats, Outing management).
  • To improve app stability and performance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your location data is shared only with members of the groups you have actively joined, and only when you have not enabled "Ghost Mode."

4. Your Choices

You can manage permissions for camera and location access at any time through your device settings. Disabling these permissions may limit your ability to use certain features of the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@company.com.
